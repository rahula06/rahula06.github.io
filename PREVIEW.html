Theoretical quantum information

Rahul Arvind

I study randomness and structure
in the quantum world.

My research interests span quantum information, many-body dynamics, and quantum cryptography. I am interested in how randomness emerges in quantum systems, how we can reproduce it efficiently, and what it makes possible.
